### HIMS-OPD-001

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| ID and title | HIMS-OPD-001 Doctor schedules and exceptions |
| Business/clinical reason | Before patients can be booked, each doctor's OPD time must be known: which department, which weekdays, which hours, how long a slot is and how many extra bookings are allowed, and when the doctor is away or the OPD is closed (spec 4.6 "doctor availability, appointment calendars"; P0 part 01 §4; D10 slots per session; D11 extra bookings). Leave already approved on the Leave page (rotas, HIMS-STAFF-012) must take sessions off without being entered twice. Owner: PO |
| Actors | Head of department and OPD scheduler (`schedules.manage` for the department; facility-wide grant for a whole-facility holiday); branch and hospital administrators (both permissions); front desk (`appointments.view`: sees sessions and free slots, not why a doctor is away); doctors (their own sessions on My schedule) |
| Preconditions | Doctors on the Staff page, posted in the department; after `migrate` (migration `scheduling.0001`) the roles hold the new permissions and the OPD scheduler role exists; feature **Appointments and outpatient visits** on (the default) |
| Main success path | Doctor schedules → department → Add weekly session (doctor, weekdays, hours, slot, extra bookings, from, until, note) → the week shows each day's sessions with their free slots → Doctor away / Holiday / Extra session → the answer lists the sessions taken off → the week shows them off (all or in part) → a session opens its free slots → Change (slots, extra, note) or End (last day, reason) → Cancel an exception brings the sessions back |
| Alternate/failure paths | Not a doctor, not working, not posted, department not active, first day in the past, slot does not fit, outside the department's opening hours, overlapping one of the doctor's sessions in any department → `409 SESSION_NOT_POSSIBLE` (or `SESSION_OVERLAP` from the database) with the reason; several weekdays all or none; exception in the past, over 366 days, times over several days, leave for a doctor not posted in the department, extra session clashing or on leave → `409 EXCEPTION_NOT_POSSIBLE`; whole-facility holiday without a facility grant → 403; changing an ended session → `409 SESSION_ENDED`; stale version → 409; feature off → `409 FEATURE_DISABLED` |
| Data effects | New tables `doctor_schedules` and `schedule_exceptions` (checks, an exclusion constraint refusing overlapping weekly sessions of one doctor, three indexes besides the foreign keys' own). Ending a posting ends the doctor's sessions there; ending employment ends all. Never deleted |
| Security classification | Internal (sessions); a doctor's away reason is Restricted (personal) and shown only to schedule keepers; no patient data. `appointments.view` is treated as patient data (calendars will show booked patients): never the supplier's support account |
| Audit requirement | `schedule.session.added`, `.changed` (before/after), `.ended` (reason), `schedule.exception.added` (kind), `.cancelled` (reason); console changes the same; area **Doctor schedules (OPD)** on the Audit log |
| Idempotency/concurrency | `Idempotency-Key` on adding sessions and exceptions; every change for one doctor locks the doctor's staff row; the database refuses overlapping weekly sessions; `version` on changes |
| Integration dependencies | None (no messages yet; patients are told with HIMS-OPD-002 / 004) |
| Acceptance criteria | A session on several weekdays appears on each with its slot count; approved leave on the Leave page takes it off without saying the kind of leave, leave asked shows "Leave asked"; part of a day away keeps the other slots on their usual times; a holiday closes a department or (with a facility grant) the whole facility; an extra session adds slots unless it clashes; the front desk sees sessions and slots but not why a doctor is away and changes nothing; a head of department sees and changes only their department; doctors see their own sessions; a posting or employment that ends ends the sessions; the console applies the same rules; the feature switch closes the screens for a facility |
| Test evidence | `apps/scheduling/tests/test_doctor_schedules.py` (13), `apps/scheduling/tests/test_schedule_invariants.py` (5: every database rule), `apps/demo/tests/test_demo_hospitals.py` (seed and `add_demo_doctor_schedules`), `apps/performance` (four queries, two screens and the console list at volume); screens `scheduling.test.tsx` (8); browser test `07-doctor-schedules.spec.ts` (3, with accessibility); manual steps [doctor schedules](../workflows/doctor-schedules.md). Reviewer: the PO, at PR review |
| Release risk | Low: new tables and screens; the only change to existing behaviour is that ending a posting or employment also ends OPD sessions. Rollback: revert the PR and `migrate scheduling zero` (the two tables are dropped). Booking into the slots: HIMS-OPD-002 |

### HIMS-OPD-002

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| ID and title | HIMS-OPD-002 Appointments without double booking, reschedule, cancel |
| Business/clinical reason | Patients must be booked into doctors' free OPD slots, moved when plans change and cancelled with the reason, and one slot must never be given to two patients, even when two receptionists book at the same moment (spec 4.6 "booking, rescheduling, cancellation"; P0 part 01 §4 and §10; part 02 "no double booking (database guarantee)"; part 03 "Appointment"; D11 extra bookings). When a doctor cannot come, the booked patients must be found at once (P0 part 01 §10). Owner: PO |
| Actors | Front desk and OPD scheduler (`appointments.manage` for the department: book, move, cancel); heads of department and branch/hospital administrators (`appointments.view`: see, do not book); medical records (restricted records); doctors (their booked count on My schedule) |
| Preconditions | Doctors' OPD sessions (HIMS-OPD-001); patients registered (HIMS-PAT-001); after `migrate` (migration `scheduling.0002`) the front desk and OPD scheduler roles hold `appointments.manage` (the scheduler also `patients.search`); feature **Appointments and outpatient visits** on |
| Main success path | Appointments → department → day → a free slot → find the patient (or come from the chart's **Book** button with the patient chosen) → visit (new / follow-up), how booked (front desk / phone), short reason → Book → the slot shows booked, the day's list has the patient → Move (another day, doctor or slot; the old one is kept as moved) or Cancel (why) → the chart's Overview shows the next appointment; the doctor's My schedule shows how many are booked |
| Alternate/failure paths | Slot taken → `409 SLOT_TAKEN` (with how many bookings over the slots are left); two bookings at the same moment → one wins, the other `409 SLOT_TAKEN` "This slot was just taken" (database rule); not a free slot of a session (off, outside it, not a slot start) → `409 NOT_A_FREE_SLOT`; in the past → `SLOT_IN_PAST`; more than 180 days ahead → `TOO_FAR`; patient already booked at that time → `PATIENT_ALREADY_BOOKED`; merged record → `PATIENT_MERGED`; over the slots into a free slot → `EXTRA_NOT_NEEDED`, none left → `EXTRA_FULL`; moving or cancelling one that is not booked → `NOT_BOOKED`; cancelling once its day is over → `ALREADY_OVER`; no reason → 400; restricted record without the right to open it → `403 RESTRICTED_RECORD`; changing a session's slot length with patients booked → `409 SESSION_HAS_BOOKINGS`; stale version → 409; feature off → `409 FEATURE_DISABLED` |
| Data effects | New table `appointments` (checks for times, one session, cancelled with when and why, not moved to itself; one replacement per appointment; an exclusion constraint refusing two appointments of one doctor at the same time; three indexes: a doctor's days, a patient's appointments, a department's booked ones). Never deleted: cancelled or moved, with who, when and why |
| Security classification | Clinical (an appointment shows which specialty a person sees, P0 part 02 "C"); the reason is short and has no history. Restricted records show as "Restricted record" with the MRN to people who may not open them. `appointments.view` and `.manage` are patient data: never the supplier's support account |
| Audit requirement | `appointment.booked` (fields, never the reason text), `.rescheduled` (before/after time and doctor, link to the new one), `.cancelled` (reason); console the same; area **Appointments (OPD)** on the Audit log |
| Idempotency/concurrency | `Idempotency-Key` on booking; a booking locks the doctor's staff row (bookings over the slots counted under it); the exclusion constraint refuses double booking past the service; move = cancel + book in one transaction; `version` on move and cancel |
| Integration dependencies | None yet: confirmations and reminders by email with HIMS-OPD-004 (needs the patient's email consent, D9) |
| Acceptance criteria | A free slot books once; the second booking of it, even at the same moment, is refused; over-the-slot bookings only into booked slots and only as many as the session allows; a moved appointment links both ways and frees its old slot; a cancelled one keeps its reason and frees its slot; the patient cannot be booked twice at the same time; a doctor away, a holiday, approved leave or an ended session lists the booked patients under To move (the exception's answer names them; the Leave page counts them first); a session's slot length cannot change under booked patients; the front desk sees "Restricted record" for restricted patients and cannot book them; heads of department see and do not book; the console books, moves (its own page), cancels (actions) and lists the patients to move; the chart shows the next appointment and a Book button |
| Test evidence | `apps/scheduling/tests/test_appointments.py` (14, including two bookings at the same moment in two threads), `apps/scheduling/tests/test_schedule_invariants.py` (7: every database rule on the three tables), `apps/demo/tests/test_demo_hospitals.py` (seed and `add_demo_appointments`), `apps/performance` (four queries and three screens plus the console list at one million appointments); screens `scheduling.test.tsx` (13); browser test `08-appointments.spec.ts` (3, with accessibility); manual steps [appointments](../workflows/appointments.md). Reviewer: the PO, at PR review |
| Release risk | Low to medium: a new table and screen; changes to existing behaviour: the week and day screens show booked counts, a session's slot length cannot change under booked patients, the Leave page shows OPD bookings, administrators no longer hold the booking permission they never used. Rollback: revert the PR and `migrate scheduling 0001` (the appointments table is dropped). Check-in, walk-ins and the queue: HIMS-OPD-003 |

### HIMS-OPD-003

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| ID and title | HIMS-OPD-003 Walk-ins, tokens and OPD queue; no-show job |
| Business/clinical reason | On the day of the visit the front desk must know who has come, in what order the doctor sees them, and who did not come; patients without an appointment must be seen too (spec 4.6 "walk-in management, token or queue management"; P0 part 01 §4 D10 tokens per doctor and day, walk-ins get the next token; D12 no-show at the end of the session, undone the same day; part 03 "Appointment"; part 06 "OPD queue (today)"). Owner: PO |
| Actors | Front desk and OPD scheduler (`appointments.manage`: check in, walk in, call next, seen, left, did not come and undo); heads of department and administrators (`appointments.view`: see the queue); medical records (restricted records); the hospital's job (no-shows) |
| Preconditions | Doctors' OPD sessions (HIMS-OPD-001) and appointments (HIMS-OPD-002); patients registered; after `migrate` (migration `scheduling.0003`); feature **Appointments and outpatient visits** on; scheduled jobs running (Celery beat or `run_scheduled_jobs`) |
| Main success path | OPD queue → department (today on the hospital's clock) → a booked patient arrives → Check in → token N, Waiting → a walk-in → Walk in (find the patient, visit, reason) → next token, no slot used → Call next → the lowest waiting token goes in to the doctor (whoever was with the doctor is seen) → Seen for the last → booked patients not checked in by the end of their session are marked Did not come by the job (or earlier by the front desk once their time has passed) → Undo before the session ends, or Check in a late patient the same day |
| Alternate/failure paths | Already checked in → `409 ALREADY_CHECKED_IN`; not booked (cancelled, moved, seen) → `NOT_BOOKED`; another day → `NOT_TODAY`; the doctor away or the session off at that time → `SESSION_OFF` (why shown only as "Away" to the front desk); walk-in without the doctor's session in the department today → `NO_SESSION_TODAY`; the patient already waiting → `ALREADY_WAITING`; booked today with that doctor → `HAS_APPOINTMENT`; merged record → `PATIENT_MERGED`; Call next with nobody → `NONE_WAITING`; Seen on someone not with the doctor → `NOT_WITH_DOCTOR`; Left on someone not waiting → `NOT_WAITING`; Did not come before its time → `NOT_YET`; Undo on one not marked → `NOT_NO_SHOW`, after the session → `SESSION_OVER`; restricted record without the right to open it (check-in, walk-in) → `403 RESTRICTED_RECORD`; stale version → 409; no `appointments.manage` → 403; feature off → `409 FEATURE_DISABLED` |
| Data effects | `appointments` gains token_number, checked_in_at, checked_in_by, called_at, seen_at; source walk_in; cancel reason left_unseen; rules: one token per doctor and day (partial unique index), a checked-in appointment has its token and time (and booked or did-not-come ones neither), with the doctor or seen means called in, seen means when, a walk-in is checked in and never over the slots; the no-double-booking rule rebuilt without walk-ins; index appointment_booked_end_idx for the job. Nothing deleted |
| Security classification | Clinical (as the appointments): the queue shows who sees which doctor today; the reason is short. Restricted records show as "Restricted record" with the MRN; checking them in needs the right to open them |
| Audit requirement | `appointment.checked_in` (token, came late or not), `.walk_in` (token), `.called`, `.seen`, `.left_unseen` (note), `.no_show` (by the front desk or the job), `.no_show_undone`; console the same; area **Appointments (OPD)** |
| Idempotency/concurrency | `Idempotency-Key` on walk-ins; check-in, walk-in and Call next lock the doctor's staff row (tokens in order, never twice; the database refuses a duplicate); the job locks each appointment and marks it only if still booked; `version` on each step |
| Integration dependencies | Scheduled job `opd-no-shows` (every 15 minutes) in `config/schedule.py`: Celery beat or the host's timer (`run_scheduled_jobs`, HIMS-OPS-023). No messages to patients (HIMS-OPD-004) |
| Acceptance criteria | Tokens follow arrival, not slot time; a walk-in gets the next token and leaves the slots free; Call next takes the lowest waiting token and ends the previous patient; a patient who leaves keeps their token on record; the job marks only booked patients of ended sessions, once, and they leave the To move list; Undo works before the session ends, a late patient is checked in after it; heads of department see the queue without buttons; restricted records stay hidden and are checked in only by those who may open them; other days show as they were; the screen asks for today on the hospital's clock; the console has the queue page, the actions and the walk-in form |
| Test evidence | `apps/scheduling/tests/test_opd_queue.py` (11), `apps/scheduling/tests/test_schedule_invariants.py` (9: every database rule, two new for the queue), `apps/demo/tests/test_demo_hospitals.py` (seed and `add_demo_opd_queue`), `apps/performance` (three queries and one screen at one million appointments), `common/tests/test_scheduled_jobs.py` (the job runs with the others); screens `scheduling.test.tsx` (18; 5 new); browser test `09-opd-queue.spec.ts` (2, with accessibility); manual steps [OPD queue](../workflows/opd-queue.md). Reviewer: the PO, at PR review |
| Release risk | Low to medium: new columns and rules on `appointments` (empty or new on every hospital); the no-double-booking rule is rebuilt (same rule, walk-ins left out); a new scheduled job. Rollback: revert the PR and `migrate scheduling 0002` (the columns and rules go; walk-ins would break the rebuilt rule, so cancel or delete walk-ins first on a test hospital). Visits and visit numbers at check-in: HIMS-CLIN-001 |
